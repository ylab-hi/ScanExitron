# Citation

How to cite ScanExitron in your research.

## Publications

If you use ScanExitron in your research, please cite our papers:

### 1. Molecular Cell (Primary Methodology)

* **Paper**: Wang, Ting-You, et al. "Molecular Cell Publication" (2021).
* **DOI**: [10.1016/j.molcel.2021.03.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2021.03.013)

## Acknowledgments

ScanExitron builds upon several open-source libraries and bioinformatics tools:

- **[regtools](https://github.com/griffithlab/regtools)**: Spliced junction extraction and annotations
- **[samtools](http://www.htslib.org/)**: BAM alignment coordinate manipulation
- **[bedtools](https://bedtools.readthedocs.io/)**: Genome arithmetic and coordinate intersections
- **[pyfaidx](https://github.com/mdshw5/pyfaidx)**: Genome FASTA querying
- **[HTSeq](https://htseq.readthedocs.io/)**: GTF genomic annotation matching
- **[pysam](https://github.com/pysam-developers/pysam)**: Python wrapper around htslib
